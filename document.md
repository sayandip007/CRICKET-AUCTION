## 1. Introduction & Overview

The **Cricket Auction Simulator** is a full-featured, authentic simulation of an Indian Premier League (IPL) mega-auction. As a franchise manager or owner, your mission is to assemble a balanced, championship-winning squad of 18 to 25 world-class cricketers within a strict ₹120.00 Crore purse.

You will encounter:
- High-stakes retention dilemmas and official price deductions.
- Multi-team bidding duels against 9 distinct AI team personas.
- Dramatic 3-stage Right to Match (RTM) card challenges.
- Live synthesized voice commentary and procedural acoustic gavel strikes.
- Post-auction team formation, chemistry calculation, and full tournament match simulations.

## 6. Right to Match (RTM) Cards Explained

If you choose not to retain all 6 players before the auction, you receive **Right to Match (RTM)** cards for the live auction pool:

$$\text{RTM Cards Allocated} = 6 - (\text{Number of Retained Players})$$

### How the 3-Stage RTM Duel Works
When a player who previously represented your franchise is sold to a rival team during the auction, the auction clock pauses and triggers the **3-Stage RTM Challenge**:

```
[Rival Franchise Wins Auction Clock at Price P]
                     │
                     ▼
       Stage 1: RTM INQUIRY
  Original Team asked: "Exercise RTM?"
         ├── Decline ──► Sold to winning bidder at price P
         └── Exercise RTM
                     │
                     ▼
       Stage 2: FINAL RAISE CHALLENGE
  Winning bidder gets ONE opportunity to raise the bid
  (e.g., raise from ₹10.00 Cr to ₹12.50 Cr)
                     │
                     ▼
       Stage 3: MATCH DECISION
  Original Team decides:
  • MATCH final bid: Player rejoins original team at final raised price!
  • PASS: Player goes to winning bidder at final raised price!
```

*Tactical Note:* The winning bidder can choose not to raise, forcing the original team to match at the existing price, or raise the stakes to make retaining the player too expensive for the former team's purse!

## 7. The Live Auction Arena & Bidding System

When the auction begins, players are called to the podium one by one. The main screen provides a live television-broadcast style display featuring the player's photo, role, age, nationality, base price, and career stats.

### 7.1 Dynamic Bid Increments
Bids automatically increase according to official IPL incremental brackets:

| Current Bid Range | Increment Step |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Below ₹1.00 Crore** | **+₹0.05 Crore** (+₹5 Lakhs) |
| **₹1.00 Crore to ₹2.00 Crore** | **+₹0.10 Crore** (+₹10 Lakhs) |
| **₹2.00 Crore to ₹5.00 Crore** | **+₹0.20 Crore** (+₹20 Lakhs) |
| **Above ₹5.00 Crore** | **+₹0.50 Crore** (+₹50 Lakhs) |

#### Quick-Jump Buttons
Want to scare off rivals or assert dominance on a marquee superstar? Use the **+₹1.00 Cr** or **+₹2.00 Cr** quick-raise buttons to jump the price instantly!

### 7.2 The 10-Second Hammer Timer & Gavel Countdown
- Each new bid resets the countdown clock to **10 seconds** (or **5 seconds** in Accelerated Rounds).
- As the timer counts down:
  - **10s – 4s**: *"Bidding Active"*
  - **3 seconds**: *"Going Once..."* (Tension tick sound & gavel elevates to $-24^\circ$)
  - **2 seconds**: *"Going Twice..."* (Urgent double tick & gavel elevates to $-48^\circ$)
  - **1 second**: *"Last Call!"* (Critical tick & gavel elevates to $-72^\circ$)
  - **0 seconds**: *"SOLD!"* (Hardwood gavel strike, confetti burst, and sale announcement)
- If you or an AI team submits a bid at the 1-second mark, the clock resets, keeping the drama alive!

### 7.3 AI Competitor Intelligence & Rivalry Bidding Wars
The 9 AI teams don't just bid randomly—they are driven by sophisticated game AI:
- **Role Quotas**: A team lacking opening batsmen or a frontline leg-spinner will aggressively bid on those positions.
- **Purse Pacing**: AI calculates remaining required slots and conserves purse so they never run out of funds prematurely.
- **Rivalry Derbies**: Historic rivalries (CSK vs MI, RCB vs KKR, CSK vs RCB) trigger high-tempo **Bidding Wars** with animated neon banners, rapid counter-bids, and tension audio!

## 14. Strategic Franchise Management: Impact Player, Trades & Multi-Year

Take complete control of franchise operations beyond the auction podium with deep tactical features:

### 14.1 Impact Player Rule (12th Man Substitution)
- **Bench Nomination**: In `🏏 Playing XI`, you can designate any bench reserve as your **Nominated Impact Player**. You can also nominate up to 5 matchday substitutes.
- **Mid-Innings Tactical Swaps**:
  - **1st Innings (Batting Reinforcement)**: If your top order stumbles or you need an explosive finish, the match engine activates the Impact Batter into the middle order to boost boundaries.
  - **2nd Innings (Target Defense / Bowling Specialist)**: Bring on a death-overs yorker specialist or mystery spinner to defend totals and contain late-innings chases.
- Substitution telemetry is tracked and detailed on every completed match scorecard.

### 14.2 Mid-Season Transfer Window & Player Trades
- Open the **Trade Center** (`🔄 Trades` in the header or tournament simulator) anytime during the tournament.
- **Player-for-Player & Cash Deals**:
  - Propose 1-for-1 or 2-for-2 swaps between your franchise and rival franchises.
  - Sweeten the deal with cash considerations (₹0.25 Cr to ₹5.00 Cr) added to or subtracted from franchise budgets.
- **Smart AI Trade Evaluations**:
  - AI franchises reject unfair offers, analyze whether the trade fills their positional deficits, and strictly enforce the 18–25 player squad boundaries and 8 overseas limit.

### 14.3 Dynamic Player Form Streaks & Fitness Fatigue
- **Form Tiers**:
  - 🔥 **On Fire (+15% boost)**: Consecutive 50+ scores or 3+ wicket hauls.
  - ⚡ **In Form (+8% boost)**: Reliable match-to-match consistency.
  - ⚪ **Standard (1.0x baseline)**: Neutral form.
  - ❄️ **In Slump (-10% penalty)**: Consecutive single-digit scores or expensive bowling spells.
  - ⚠️ **Fatigued (-14% penalty)**: Bowlers carrying heavy workloads over consecutive matches.
- **Resting & Rotation**: Bench fatigued players for a match or two to restore stamina and avoid season-ending slumps!

### 14.4 Multi-Year Franchise Mode & Annual Mini-Auctions
- **Carry Forward Your Legacy**: Once an IPL champion is crowned, advance to **Season 2** and **Season 3** (`🚀 S2 Mini-Auction`).
- **Retentions & Purse Recovery**:
  - Retain up to 15–18 core stars.
  - Release underperforming players to recover their contract money back into your auction purse!
- **Rookie Draft Class**:
  - Bid on newly added high-ceiling prodigies (U-19 sensations, express 150km/h pacers, mystery spinners, and clutch finishers) in a fast-paced mini-auction!
