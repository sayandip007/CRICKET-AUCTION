## 1. Introduction & Overview

The **Cricket Auction Simulator** is a full-featured, authentic simulation of an Indian Premier League (IPL) mega-auction. As a franchise manager or owner, your mission is to assemble a balanced, championship-winning squad of 18 to 25 world-class cricketers within a strict ₹120.00 Crore purse.

You will encounter:
- High-stakes retention dilemmas and official price deductions.
- Multi-team bidding duels against 9 distinct AI team personas.
- Dramatic 3-stage Right to Match (RTM) card challenges.
- Live synthesized voice commentary and procedural acoustic gavel strikes.
- Post-auction team formation, chemistry calculation, and full tournament match simulations.

## 3. Game Modes: Solo vs. Pass & Play Multiplayer

### 3.1 Solo Manager (1 Human vs. 9 AI Teams)
- You take full ownership of your chosen franchise.
- You personally decide retentions, place manual bids, trigger quick-raise jumps, and exercise your RTM cards.
- The remaining 9 franchises are operated by the simulator's target-aware AI engine, evaluating bids based on squad balance, role deficits, purse-pacing, and historic rivalries.

### 3.2 Pass-and-Play Multiplayer (2 to 10 Human Managers)
- Perfect for friends playing together on one computer, laptop, or tablet!
- In the Team Selection screen, switch to **👥 Pass-and-Play Multiplayer**.
- Pick any combination of franchises (e.g., CSK vs. MI derby, "Big 4", or all 10 franchises).
- Each manager can set their personal name in the **Multi-Manager Settings Modal**.
- Pre-auction retentions are processed sequentially for each human team in a clear queue.
- During the live auction:
  - An **Active Paddle Selector** lets you switch which team places the next bid with the main buttons.
  - Dedicated **Direct Multi-Paddles** allow any human manager to raise their paddle instantly without switching tabs!
  - When RTM situations arise involving human teams, interactive inquiry and final-raise challenge modals guide each player through their decision.

## 5. Pre-Auction Player Retentions & Slabs

Before the live hammer drops, franchises can lock in up to **6 key players** from their previous season squad according to official IPL retention guidelines:

### Retention Caps
- **Maximum Total Retentions**: 6 players
- **Maximum Capped Players**: 5 (Indian or Overseas)
- **Maximum Uncapped Players**: 2 (Indian Uncapped only)

### Official Retention Deduction Slabs
Retaining players directly deducts money from your starting ₹120.00 Cr purse:

| Retention Slot | Player Classification | Purse Deduction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retention 1** | Capped International / Indian | **₹18.00 Crore** |
| **Retention 2** | Capped International / Indian | **₹14.00 Crore** |
| **Retention 3** | Capped International / Indian | **₹11.00 Crore** |
| **Retention 4** | Capped International / Indian | **₹18.00 Crore** |
| **Retention 5** | Capped International / Indian | **₹14.00 Crore** |
| **Uncapped Slot** | Uncapped Indian | **₹4.00 Crore flat** |

*Example:* If CSK retains Ruturaj Gaikwad (Slot 1, ₹18 Cr), Ravindra Jadeja (Slot 2, ₹14 Cr), and Shivam Dube (Slot 3, ₹11 Cr), total deduction = ₹43 Cr. CSK enters the auction with ₹77.00 Cr remaining and **3 RTM cards**.

## 6. Right to Match (RTM) Cards Explained

If you choose not to retain all 6 players before the auction, you receive **Right to Match (RTM)** cards for the live auction pool:

$$\text{RTM Cards Allocated} = 6 - (\text{Number of Retained Players})$$

### How the 3-Stage RTM Duel Works
When a player who previously represented your franchise is sold to a rival team during the auction, the auction clock pauses and triggers the **3-Stage RTM Challenge**:

```
[Rival Franchise Wins Auction Clock at Price P]
                     │
                     ▼
       Stage 1: RTM INQUIRY
  Original Team asked: "Exercise RTM?"
         ├── Decline ──► Sold to winning bidder at price P
         └── Exercise RTM
                     │
                     ▼
       Stage 2: FINAL RAISE CHALLENGE
  Winning bidder gets ONE opportunity to raise the bid
  (e.g., raise from ₹10.00 Cr to ₹12.50 Cr)
                     │
                     ▼
       Stage 3: MATCH DECISION
  Original Team decides:
  • MATCH final bid: Player rejoins original team at final raised price!
  • PASS: Player goes to winning bidder at final raised price!
```

*Tactical Note:* The winning bidder can choose not to raise, forcing the original team to match at the existing price, or raise the stakes to make retaining the player too expensive for the former team's purse!

## 7. The Live Auction Arena & Bidding System

When the auction begins, players are called to the podium one by one. The main screen provides a live television-broadcast style display featuring the player's photo, role, age, nationality, base price, and career stats.

### 7.1 Dynamic Bid Increments
Bids automatically increase according to official IPL incremental brackets:

| Current Bid Range | Increment Step |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Below ₹1.00 Crore** | **+₹0.05 Crore** (+₹5 Lakhs) |
| **₹1.00 Crore to ₹2.00 Crore** | **+₹0.10 Crore** (+₹10 Lakhs) |
| **₹2.00 Crore to ₹5.00 Crore** | **+₹0.20 Crore** (+₹20 Lakhs) |
| **Above ₹5.00 Crore** | **+₹0.50 Crore** (+₹50 Lakhs) |

#### Quick-Jump Buttons
Want to scare off rivals or assert dominance on a marquee superstar? Use the **+₹1.00 Cr** or **+₹2.00 Cr** quick-raise buttons to jump the price instantly!

### 7.2 The 10-Second Hammer Timer & Gavel Countdown
- Each new bid resets the countdown clock to **10 seconds** (or **5 seconds** in Accelerated Rounds).
- As the timer counts down:
  - **10s – 4s**: *"Bidding Active"*
  - **3 seconds**: *"Going Once..."* (Tension tick sound & gavel elevates to $-24^\circ$)
  - **2 seconds**: *"Going Twice..."* (Urgent double tick & gavel elevates to $-48^\circ$)
  - **1 second**: *"Last Call!"* (Critical tick & gavel elevates to $-72^\circ$)
  - **0 seconds**: *"SOLD!"* (Hardwood gavel strike, confetti burst, and sale announcement)
- If you or an AI team submits a bid at the 1-second mark, the clock resets, keeping the drama alive!

### 7.3 AI Competitor Intelligence & Rivalry Bidding Wars
The 9 AI teams don't just bid randomly—they are driven by sophisticated game AI:
- **Role Quotas**: A team lacking opening batsmen or a frontline leg-spinner will aggressively bid on those positions.
- **Purse Pacing**: AI calculates remaining required slots and conserves purse so they never run out of funds prematurely.
- **Rivalry Derbies**: Historic rivalries (CSK vs MI, RCB vs KKR, CSK vs RCB) trigger high-tempo **Bidding Wars** with animated neon banners, rapid counter-bids, and tension audio!

Players are presented in structured, realistic sets:
1. **Marquee Set 1 (M1)**: Superstars & generational match-winners (Pant, Rohit, Kohli, Bumrah, Starc, etc.)
2. **Marquee Set 2 (M2)**: Elite international and Indian captains and match-winners
3. **Capped Batsmen (BA1)**
4. **Capped All-Rounders (AL1)**
5. **Capped Wicketkeepers (WK1)**
6. **Capped Fast Bowlers (FA1)**
7. **Capped Spinners (SP1)**
8. **Uncapped Sets (UBA, UAL, UWK, UFA, USP)**: Exciting young domestic talent at lower base prices (₹30L – ₹40L)

### Accelerated Round (Unsold Recall)
When all regular sets conclude:
- Any player who went "UNSOLD" is compiled into the **Accelerated Round Shortlist**.
- You and AI teams can recall unsold players.
- Recalled players receive a **50% discount** on their base price and are auctioned with a rapid **5-second clock**!

## 10. Playing XI Builder & Squad Chemistry Rating

Once you've built your squad (or anytime during the auction via `🏏 Playing XI` in the top bar):

1. **Pick Your Starting 11**:
   - Choose 11 players from your signed squad.
   - Designate a **Captain (C)** and **Vice-Captain (VC)**.
   - Set the batting order (1 to 11).
2. **Formation Regulations**:
   - Must contain **at least 1 Wicketkeeper**.
   - Must contain **at least 5 bowling options** (Bowlers or All-Rounders).
   - Can field **at most 4 overseas players** (enforcing official IPL playing conditions).
3. **Squad Chemistry Score ($0\text{–}100$)**:
   - Evaluates your **Batting Power**, **Bowling Penetration**, **Balance & Versatility**, and **Big-Match Experience**.
   - Assigns a tiered medal: **S-Tier (Championship Contender)**, **A-Tier (Playoff Contender)**, or **B/C-Tier (Rebuilding Squad)**.

## 14. Pro Tips & Championship Winning Strategies

1. **Protect Your Purse Reserves**: Don't blow all your money in Marquee Set 1. The middle sets (Set 3–6) often offer incredible value for elite pacers and all-rounders at a fraction of superstar prices.
2. **Master the RTM Mindset**: Don't waste early retentions on players you can buy back cheaper at the auction. Retaining a player at ₹18 Cr when market value might be ₹11 Cr loses you ₹7 Cr in bidding power!
3. **Use the +₹1 Cr and +₹2 Cr Quick-Jump**: When an AI team is bidding slowly (+₹10L), an aggressive jump of +₹1.50 Cr or +₹2.00 Cr can immediately push the bid past their ceiling, securing the player instantly.
4. **Scout Team Needs Before Bidding**: Click `🎯 Needs & Intel` to see what roles your rivals are targeting. If you notice KKR desperately needs a wicketkeeper, you can drive up the price on their targets or snipe the player first!
5. **Mind the 8 Overseas Ceiling**: Keep careful count of international signings. Once you reach 8 overseas players, you cannot bid on any foreign talent, even at base price!
6. **Balance Your Playing XI**: When bidding, always ensure you have a designated wicketkeeper and at least 5 reliable bowling options to unlock maximum squad chemistry.
